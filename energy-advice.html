Independent energy advice

Before you buy anything, compare the options properly.

A solar installer can tell you what they can sell you. We can independently model different panel layouts, battery capacities and electricity tariffs to explore what actually makes sense for your home.

The questions worth answering

Does the bigger system actually earn its keep?

Panel layout

Compare panel number, capacity, orientation and expected generation rather than relying only on headline system size.

Battery capacity

Compare smaller and larger storage options against the household's actual demand and likely solar surplus.

Tariffs

Model import and export tariffs, including time-of-use pricing, because when energy is bought and sold can change the answer.
