Energy optimisation

Your battery can do more than store solar.

The difficult part is not charging a home battery. It is deciding when to charge it, when to keep energy for later, when to power the house and when exporting might make more sense.

The idea

Plan energy around what tomorrow is likely to look like.

An optimisation system can combine changing electricity prices, predicted solar generation, expected household demand and the current state of the battery. It then keeps recalculating a plan as those inputs change.

What powers this?

PredBat is one tool we can use.

PredBat is an open-source home-energy optimisation system that works with Home Assistant and compatible battery systems. It can calculate charging and discharging plans using tariff prices, predicted solar generation, expected household consumption and the capabilities of the inverter and battery.

PredBat itself is not the service. The useful work is understanding the home, integrating compatible equipment, validating the data, configuring the optimisation, commissioning it properly and making sure the household can understand what it is doing.
