Our Guildford test house

What does a genuinely smart home do?

A real family house where heating, lighting, energy, appliances, blinds, security and routines have gradually learned to work together.

Comfort

Lights that know it's 3am

The same movement can produce normal lighting during the day and a very low level at night.

Heating

Heat the rooms that actually need it

Individual rooms can call for heat independently, so the central system responds to real demand.

Efficiency

Dry the clothes, not the room

Power use and humidity can determine whether clothes are still drying and stop equipment when the job is done.

Routine

A gentler start to the morning

A household schedule can coordinate blinds, lights and music into one wake-up routine.

Awareness

Notice unusual behaviour

Existing energy data can reveal when an appliance behaves abnormally and trigger a useful warning.

Existing equipment

Make the house you already own smart

Garage doors, alarms and other existing equipment can often be monitored and controlled without wholesale replacement.

And occasionally, because we can
The office light follows live Formula 1 track status.

Green track. Yellow flag. Safety car. Virtual safety car. Red flag. Not every automation needs a business case.

One system, many manufacturers

Make sense of what you've already bought.

Heating, solar, battery storage, appliances, lighting, blinds, security, media, phones and other systems can work together without expecting the household to manage each one separately.
